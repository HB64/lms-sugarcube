PLUGIN_SUGARCUBE
	EN	SugarCube
PLUGIN_SUGARCUBE_DESCINSTALL
	EN	SC provides an interface to MusicIP's mixing engine. In Chain mode it queues one track at a time, each chosen to follow the one before it like a journey, based on acoustic similarity, tags, ratings and play history as set by you. It can also generate a Batch based on a single track, album or mood. Live controls and a sleep timer are included.

PLUGIN_SC_WEB_WOBBLE
	EN	SugarCube Wobble
PLUGIN_SC_WEB_WOBBLEDESC
	EN	Normally the single best MusicIP match is always used as the next track in the Chain. Wobble instead picks randomly from a window at the top of the list, so playback doesn't always take the single most similar path: Tight Wobble (first 3), Medium Wobble (first 5) or Loose Wobble (any track). Floating Wobble re-rolls between those three every time a track is picked.
